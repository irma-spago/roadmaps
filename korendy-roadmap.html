04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone dealing with clogged pores needs a guide, while someone choosing a Korean sunscreen needs a listicle or a collection page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • How to unclog clogged pores
  • Melasma
  • Keratosis pilaris
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one for their skin.

Example topics

  • Best moisturizers for dry skin
  • Best mineral sunscreens
  • Best hand creams
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Korendy products with buying advice for that need.

Example topics

  • Non-comedogenic face cleansers
  • K-beauty vitamin C serums
  • Korean SPF sunscreens
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for readers choosing between ingredients, formulas or treatments.

Example topics

  • Retinol vs. tretinoin
  • Mineral vs. chemical sunscreen
  • Dandruff vs. dry scalp
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a product type, explaining what it does, who it suits and how to use it.

Example topics

  • Air cushion foundation
  • Magnesium niacinamide cream
  • Bio-active ceramide moisturizer
Tool

Tools & finders

Hands-on pages people use rather than read: undertone, shade and routine finders that point to the right products.
