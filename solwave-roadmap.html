04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone wondering how red light works needs a guide, while someone choosing a mask needs a listicle or a product page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Red light therapy for skin health
  • How to remove dark circles
  • How to brighten dull skin
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one for their skin.

Example topics

  • Best face wrinkle creams
  • Best hydrating serums
  • Best red light therapy masks
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers choosing between devices, ingredients or products.

Example topics

  • Niacinamide vs. vitamin C
  • Red light wand vs. mask
  • Eye serum vs. eye cream
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a device or product, explaining what it does, who it suits and how to use it.

Example topics

  • At-home LED face masks
  • SolaWave Eye Recovery Pro mask
  • SolaWave conductive serum
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right SolaWave devices and skincare with buying advice.

Example topics

  • At-home beauty devices
  • Hydrating face mists
  • Red light therapy serums
Recipe

Recipes

Simple DIY recipes for serums, cleansers and creams, for readers who like to make some of their skincare at home.

Example topics

  • DIY hyaluronic acid serum
  • Homemade glowing-skin face wash
  • DIY hyaluronic acid moisturizer
