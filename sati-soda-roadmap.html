04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone curious about an ingredient needs a guide, while someone planning a party needs a recipe or a list of drinks to try.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • Ashwagandha benefits
  • What is monk fruit?
  • Caffeine withdrawal
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the ones that suit them.

Example topics

  • Best non-alcoholic drinks
  • Healthiest soda
  • Winter mocktail recipes
Recipe

Recipes

Step-by-step drink recipes with ingredients and method, built for people looking for something to make tonight.

Example topics

  • Shirley Temple mocktail
  • Non-alcoholic cranberry punch
  • Margarita mocktail
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers deciding between drinks, sweeteners or ingredients.

Example topics

  • Is diet soda better than regular soda?
  • Allulose vs. stevia vs. monk fruit
  • Coffee vs. energy drinks
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Sati Soda products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Mocktail recipes
  • Zero-sugar energy drinks
  • Organic functional beverages
Location

Location pages

Pages for local searches that tell readers where to find functional and CBD drinks in their city or state.

Example topics

  • Where to buy adaptogen drinks
  • CBD drinks in Chicago
  • CBD drinks in Florida
