04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone making soup tonight needs a recipe, while someone stocking up on flour needs a collection page.

Recipe

Recipes

Step-by-step recipes with ingredients and method, built around the bulk foods people cook with every day.

Example topics

  • Homemade hummus
  • Lentil bean soup
  • Molasses cookies
Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • What is nutritional yeast?
  • How to cook dried beans
  • How to feed a sourdough starter
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Country Life products with buying advice.

Example topics

  • Gluten-free flour blends
  • Medjool dates
  • Chickpeas
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of foods and products that let readers scan the options and pick what fits them.

Example topics

  • High-fiber foods for constipation
  • Natural sweeteners
  • Healthiest nut butters
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers choosing between grains, flours, nuts or milks.

Example topics

  • Steel-cut vs. rolled oats
  • Almond meal vs. almond flour
  • Dairy-free milks compared
Product

Product & wholesale pages

Pages for searches that name a product or buy in bulk, linking to the product, bulk sizes and wholesale prices.

Example topics

  • Oregon Trail sourdough starter
  • Wholesale Medjool dates
  • Wholesale dried fruit
