04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone planting tulips this fall needs a guide, while someone choosing bulbs needs a collection or product page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Tulip planting and care
  • Amaryllis care
  • How to grow ranunculus
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Longfield bulbs and plants with buying advice.

Example topics

  • Daffodil bulbs
  • Ranunculus corms
  • Hyacinth bulbs
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a variety, showing its color, bloom time and height, and how to plant it.

Example topics

  • Saffron crocus bulbs
  • Stargazer lily bulbs
  • Rainbow Parrot tulips
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of varieties and plants that let gardeners scan the options and pick what fits.

Example topics

  • Shade perennials
  • Iris flower varieties
  • Clematis varieties
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two similar plants or bulb types, for gardeners deciding which one to grow.

Example topics

  • Grape hyacinth vs. hyacinth
  • Iris bulbs vs. rhizomes
  • Autumn vs. spring crocus
Zone

Zone & regional guides

Planting guides for a hardiness zone or state, telling gardeners exactly when and how to plant locally.

Example topics

  • Ranunculus in zone 7
  • Planting tulips in Texas
  • Shade perennials for zone 8
