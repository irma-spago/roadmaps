04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone wondering why they feel bloated needs a guide, while someone choosing a moisturizer needs a listicle.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • Diet for constipation
  • What is a calorie deficit?
  • How to heal dry, cracked feet
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the foods, products or scents that suit them.

Example topics

  • Best probiotics
  • Foods highest in antioxidants
  • Floral perfumes
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for readers trying to understand how two things differ or which one fits.

Example topics

  • Digestive bitters vs. digestive enzymes
  • Lymphocytes vs. phagocytes
  • Hematopoiesis vs. erythropoiesis
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Maison Beljanski products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Antioxidant supplements
  • Immune support supplements
  • Organic skincare
Tool

Tools & worksheets

Hands-on pages people use rather than read: calculators, diaries and worksheets that help them track what is going on.

Example topics

  • Food diary for food intolerance
  • Calorie deficit calculator
  • Mindful eating worksheets
Service

Coaching & services

Pages for people looking for personal guidance, linking to Maison Beljanski's health coaching and personalized plans.

Example topics

  • Health coaching for weight loss
  • Women's health coaching
  • Liver health programs
