04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone wondering what methylation is needs a guide, while someone choosing a form of magnesium needs a comparison.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • What is methylation in the body?
  • How to clear brain fog
  • Sleep hygiene tips
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or more options, for readers deciding between supplement forms, ingredients or approaches.

Example topics

  • Magnesium glycinate vs. citrate
  • The four forms of vitamin B12 compared
  • MTHFR homozygous vs. heterozygous
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the supplements or techniques that suit them.

Example topics

  • Best energy supplements
  • Relaxation techniques
  • Best vitamin B complex for adults
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Otherway products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Methylation supplements
  • Nootropic products
  • MTHFR supplements in Australia
Product

Product & dose pages

Pages for searches that name a specific supplement or dose, explaining what it does, who it suits and how much to take.

Example topics

  • Magnesium sleep supplement
  • Phosphatidylserine 300 mg
  • B12 cyanocobalamin 1,000 mcg
Tool

Tools & recipes

Hands-on pages people use rather than read: checks they can do at home and simple recipes that fit a supplement into the day.

Example topics

  • How to test sleep quality at home
  • Magnesium hot chocolate for sleep
  • Magnesium mocktail for sleep
