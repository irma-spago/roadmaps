04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone struggling with a shallow latch needs a guide, while someone choosing bottle nipples needs a listicle.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • How to use a nipple shield
  • Cluster feeding
  • How to store expressed breast milk
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the products or ideas that suit them.

Example topics

  • Best bottle nipples for breastfed babies
  • Best breast milk coolers
  • 7-day breastfeeding diet plan
Recipe

Recipes

Step-by-step lactation recipes with ingredients and method, for moms who want to bake or blend their own.

Example topics

  • Oatmeal chocolate chip lactation cookies
  • Lactation bars
  • Lactation smoothies
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for readers deciding between feeding approaches, herbs or products.

Example topics

  • Exclusive pumping vs. breastfeeding
  • Colostrum vs. breast milk
  • Fresh vs. frozen breast milk
Collection

Collection & product pages

Category and product pages for shopping searches that pair the right Milky Mama products with buying advice.

Example topics

  • Lactation cookies
  • Lactation drinks
  • Fenugreek-free lactation supplements
Local

Services & local pages

Pages for moms looking for hands-on help or a place to pump: lactation consultants, classes and lactation rooms nearby.

Example topics

  • Private lactation consultants
