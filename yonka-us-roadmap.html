04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone learning to build a routine needs a guide, while someone choosing a vitamin C serum needs a listicle or a collection page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • How to build a skincare routine
  • Whiteheads
  • How to exfoliate your body
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one that suits their skin.

Example topics

  • Best vitamin C serums
  • Best moisturizers for dry skin
  • Best self-tanners
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Yon-Ka products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Body oils
  • Micellar water
  • Anti-wrinkle face serums
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers deciding between ingredients, products or formats.

Example topics

  • AHA vs. BHA vs. PHA
  • Beard oil vs. beard balm
  • Body butter vs. lotion vs. oil
Recipe

Recipes

Step-by-step DIY recipes with ingredients and method, for readers who like to make their own scrubs, balms and removers.

Example topics

  • Exfoliating sugar scrub
  • Beard balm
  • DIY makeup remover
Treatment

Treatment pages

Pages for searches about professional treatments, explaining what happens, who it suits and where to book one at a Yon-Ka spa.

Example topics

  • Acid peels for hyperpigmentation
  • Professional face exfoliation
  • Chemical peels for dull skin
