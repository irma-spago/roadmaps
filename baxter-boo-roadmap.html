04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone shopping for a cat tree needs a collection page, while someone crate training a puppy needs a guide.

Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right products from 500+ brands with buying advice.

Example topics

  • Cat trees
  • Heartworm prevention for dogs
  • Dog sweaters
Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Dog crate training tips
  • Puppy vaccination schedule
  • How to size a dog harness
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products and gifts that let readers scan the options and pick what fits their pet.

Example topics

  • Memorial pet gifts
  • Best dog joint supplements
  • Best dog harnesses
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for owners choosing between foods, gear, tools or treatments.

Example topics

Recipes
