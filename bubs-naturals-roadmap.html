04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone planning a workout needs a guide, while someone choosing a creamer needs a listicle or a product page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Creatine monohydrate benefits
  • Progressive overload
  • Electrolyte drinks for diarrhea
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products, foods and ideas that let readers scan the options and pick what fits them.

Example topics

  • Vitamin C rich foods
  • Best coconut creamers
  • Best electrolyte drinks
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for readers deciding between supplements, ingredients or training methods.

Example topics

  • Whey protein vs. collagen
  • Hypertrophy vs. strength training
  • Coconut creamer vs. coconut milk
Recipe

Recipes

Step-by-step recipes for drinks and coffees, with ingredients and method, for readers who like to make their own.

Example topics

  • Homemade electrolyte drink
  • Protein coffee at home
  • Apple cider vinegar drink
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a product type, explaining what it does, who it suits and how to use it well.

Example topics

  • Grass-fed collagen peptides
  • Handheld milk frothers
  • BUBS Naturals MCT oil powder
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right BUBS Naturals products with buying advice.
