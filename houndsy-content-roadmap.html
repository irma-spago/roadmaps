04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone setting up a puppy's routine needs a guide, while someone choosing a storage container needs a listicle.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Puppy feeding schedule by age
  • Dog food shelf life
  • Switching dog food
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one for their dog.

Example topics

  • Best dog food storage containers
  • Best stainless steel dog bowls
  • Best elevated dog bowls
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers choosing between bowls, foods or feeding styles.

Example topics

  • Ceramic vs. stainless steel bowls
  • Dry vs. wet vs. raw dog food
  • Free vs. scheduled feeding
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a product type, showing what it does, who it suits and where Houndsy fits in.

Example topics

  • Manual dog food dispensers
  • Dog bowl storage cabinets
  • Large mats for dog bowls
DIY

DIY guides

Step-by-step builds for stands, cabinets and dispensers, for owners who like to make their own feeding setup.

Example topics

  • DIY dog bowl stand
  • DIY dog food storage cabinet
  • DIY dog food dispenser
Tool

Tools & calculators

Hands-on pages people use rather than read: calculators and reminders that help get portions and timing right.

Example topics

  • Cups of dog food per pound
  • Dog body condition score
  • Dog feeding reminders
