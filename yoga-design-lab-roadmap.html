04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone learning a technique needs a guide, while someone choosing a mat needs a collection page or a comparison.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to how-to and what-is questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • Self-myofascial release
  • Post-workout muscle soreness
  • Vinyasa yoga
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Yoga Design Lab products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Yoga mats
  • Yoga blocks
  • Yoga bolsters
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the ones that suit them.

Example topics

  • Best-rated yoga mats
  • Partner yoga poses for beginners
  • 26 yin yoga poses
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers deciding between styles, materials or sizes.

Example topics

  • Hatha yoga vs. vinyasa yoga
  • TPE vs. PVC yoga mats
  • 3mm vs. 6mm yoga mats
Product

Product pages

Pages built around a single product, answering the questions shoppers ask before they buy it.

Example topics

  • Cork massage ball
  • Yoga mat cleaner
  • Cork yoga blocks, set of 2
Service

Service pages

Pages for studios, gyms and businesses buying in volume, covering wholesale orders and custom branding.

Example topics

  • Bulk yoga mats
