04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone learning a technique needs a guide, while someone choosing a mat needs a collection page or a comparison.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to how-to and what-is questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • How to use a foam roller
  • Chair yoga
  • Yin yoga explained
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Hugger Mugger products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Yoga mats
  • Foam rollers
  • Yoga blocks
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the ones that suit them.

Example topics

  • Relaxation techniques
  • Best-rated yoga mats
  • Mindfulness exercises
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers deciding between styles, materials or methods.

Example topics

  • Hatha vs. vinyasa vs. yin yoga
  • Myofascial release vs. massage
  • TPE vs. PVC yoga mats
Product

Product pages

Pages built around a single product, answering the questions shoppers ask before they buy it.

Example topics

  • Tingsha bells
  • Zafu cushions
  • Heated neck wraps
Practice

Guided practices

Timed sessions readers follow along with, such as yoga nidra of a set length or for a time of year.

Example topics

  • 20-minute yoga nidra
  • 30-minute yoga nidra
  • Yoga nidra for the winter solstice
