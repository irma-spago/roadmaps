04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone dealing with razor burn needs a guide, while someone choosing a deodorant needs a listicle or a collection page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as a section, so one page covers it all.

Example topics

  • Razor burn
  • Vaginal odor
  • Is aluminum in deodorant safe?
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Honestly pHresh products with buying advice.

Example topics

  • Feminine wash
  • Natural deodorant for men
  • Baking soda-free deodorant
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one that suits them.

Example topics

  • Best aluminum-free deodorants
  • Best feminine washes
  • Best hypoallergenic deodorants
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two options, for readers deciding between deodorant types, ingredients or treatments.

Example topics

  • Antiperspirant vs. deodorant
  • Spray vs. stick deodorant
  • Magnesium vs. aluminum deodorant
Recipe

Recipes

Simple DIY recipes for deodorants, balms and powders, for readers who like to make some of their products at home.

Example topics

  • Baking soda-free deodorant recipe
  • DIY aftershave balm
  • DIY foot odor powder
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a product type, explaining what it does, who it suits and how to use it well.

Example topics

  • Unscented feminine wash
  • Hypoallergenic feminine wash
  • Aftershave oil
