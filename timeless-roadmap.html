04 - Page types
How each page is shaped

Six kinds of pages, each matched to the search behind it

Each topic gets the format that fits what people want when they search it. Someone wondering why their skin looks dull needs a guide, while someone choosing an eye cream needs a listicle or a collection page.

Guide

Guides & explainers

In-depth answers to what-is and how-to questions, with each subtopic as its own section, so one page covers the whole question.

Example topics

  • How to fix uneven skin tone
  • How to get rid of eye bags and puffiness
  • What are peptides in skincare?
Listicle

Listicles

Ranked or grouped lists of products that let readers scan the options quickly and pick the one that suits their skin.

Example topics

  • Best rated eye creams for dark circles
  • Best lightweight moisturizers
  • Best sunscreens for hyperpigmentation
Comparison

Comparisons

Side-by-side looks at two or three options, for readers deciding between ingredients, products or treatments.

Example topics

  • Peptides vs. retinol
  • Niacinamide vs. vitamin C
  • Skin purging vs. breakouts
Collection

Collection pages

Category pages for shopping searches that pair the right Timeless products with buying advice for that category.

Example topics

  • Vitamin C facial serums
  • Anti-wrinkle face serums
  • Eye creams
Product

Product pages

Pages for searches that name a specific product, explaining what it does, who it suits and how to use it.

Example topics

  • Hyaluronic acid serum
  • Timeless CoQ10 serum
  • Argan facial oil
Routine

Routine builders

Step-by-step routines for a skin type, goal or season, showing which products to use, in what order and when.

Example topics

  • Night skincare routine
  • Skincare routine for dry skin
  • Personalised skincare routine
